Cogwild Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Cogwild is made by BlueDucky Games · Contact: theblueduckymc@gmail.com

Short version

  • You can play without an account. Then your progress stays on your device only.
  • Without an account we store a random code for your device and your coins, level, unlocks and pass progress on our server, so the Shop works the same for everyone. It is forgotten after 6 months without playing.
  • With an account we store your email address (only to send you login codes; other players never see it and we never send anything else), your username, a scrambled (hashed) password, your game progress (including coins and unlocked items), trophies and ranked points, your friends list, the club you're in, and your chat messages to friends (the last 60 per friend). If you sign up with Google we get your Google email address only (not your name, photo or contacts). We never ask for your real name, phone number, location or photos.
  • Voice chat is off until you turn it on. Your voice goes live to the players you talk with (through our relay server, so they never see your internet address) and is never recorded or stored.
  • There are no ads from us and we never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time in the game (Profile → Delete account).

Playing online

In online matches other players see your username (or pilot name if you play as a guest), avatar, trophies, your mech and what your mech does in the match. Friends can watch your online matches (you see a note when they do). In matches, chat is limited to a fixed list of quick messages (like "GG!"). With your friends, party and club you can type your own messages, unless you (or a parent) turn on Quick chat only in Settings › Chat: then you only send and see the fixed messages and voice chat is off. Tap any name in the chat to block (nothing from them reaches you, also no voice) or report a player. Friend requests and party invites only come from players who know your exact username, and you choose whether to accept them.

Voice chat (optional, needs an account): the game asks first, and only uses your microphone while voice chat is on. You choose whether you hear only your party or also the players in your match, and you can mute any time (M, or tap 🎤). Voice goes through our relay server so other players never learn your internet address, and it is never recorded.

On your device

The game saves your progress and settings in your browser's or app's local storage. Guests' progress never leaves the device unless they copy a save or blueprint code themselves. Clips (F8) are saved straight to your device and are never uploaded.

Who we share data with

Nobody. Our server runs on Oracle Cloud (United States), which only stores it for us. If you play on a game portal (for example CrazyGames, Poki or itch.io) or install the app from a store (for example Google Play), that service may collect data under its own privacy policy and may show its own ads.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Profile → 🗑 Delete account, then type your password. Your account, trophies, coins and unlocks, friends, club membership and cloud save are removed right away. Server backups are kept for at most 14 days and then disappear.
  • On the web: open cogwild.duckdns.org, log in and use Profile → Delete account (works from any computer or phone).
  • By email: forgot your password? Email us from the address in this policy with your username and we will delete the account.
  • Guests: clear the game's site data, or use Settings → Reset all progress.

Children

Cogwild is made for everyone, including kids. We designed it to collect as little as possible: no real names, no photos and no location. Typed chat is only with friends, party and club, and Quick chat only (Settings › Chat, saved on the account) limits a child to fixed messages and turns voice chat off. Players can be blocked and reported at any time. Usernames must not contain your real name; we may rename or remove usernames that do or that are rude. If you are under 13 (or the age your country requires), please ask a parent before making an account. Parents can ask us to delete their child's account at the email above.

Security

The game talks to our server over encrypted HTTPS / WSS connections. Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes. Nothing is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California) you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and mention it in the game's "What's new".
