Short version

Cogwild does not have accounts, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send your game data to us. Your progress is saved only in your own browser.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (level, coins, unlocks, settings, achievements and the mechs you build) in your browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you copy a save or blueprint code yourself. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or with Settings → Reset all progress.

The "pilot name" you can type in the menu is stored the same way and is only shown to you.

Clips

The clip recorder creates video files from the game screen only when you press F8. The file is saved straight to your device's Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Web game portals and ads

If you play Cogwild on a game portal (for example CrazyGames or Poki), that portal may show ads and may collect data under its own privacy policy. Please read the portal's privacy policy. Cogwild itself does not collect advertising data.

Third-party code

The game loads the open-source 3D library three.js and fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any website.

Children

Cogwild is made for a general audience. It has no chat, no accounts and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If online multiplayer or accounts are added later, this policy will be updated before those features go live.

Cogwild is an independent game. This policy is a starting template: have it checked before a commercial launch.
