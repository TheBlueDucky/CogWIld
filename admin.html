🛡 Cogwild moderation

How it works: players report others from a name in the chat or a mech in the gallery. When 3 different players report someone's chat within a day, their typed chat turns off for 24 hours by itself until you look. Mute turns off typed chat (quick chat still works). Ban signs the account out and stops it logging in. Quick chat only makes the account use ready-made lines (the player can't switch it back). Stats counts each player once a day (accounts and guests, no names kept); "came back" = new players who played again the next day / a week later. Bugs are errors games sent by themselves; mark one fixed after an update, and it comes back marked "happened again" if it wasn't.
