## Short description (itch.io / portals, ~300 characters)
Snap parts together into tanks, spiders, boats, planes or all three at once, then battle bots in Rush Build (build from scratch in 75 seconds!) or Workshop Clash (bring your best design). First-person cockpit view, dashes, spike rams, power-ups and two wild arenas.

## Long description
**Build anything. Break everything.**

Cogwild is a mech-building battle game. Snap together wheels, legs, floats, propellers, wings, rotors, jets, cannons, rockets, lasers, spikes and shields, and your design decides how you fight.

- **Your design is your strategy**: wheels are fast, legs climb cliffs, floats sail, rotors hover, wings and jets fly like a plane.
- **Mix movement types** as you rank up: Mono → Hybrid → Omni. A tank that dives AND flies? Earn it.
- **Rush Build**: everyone starts with just a Core and 75 seconds. Chaos guaranteed.
- **Workshop Clash**: bring the mech you perfected in the Workshop.
- **1v1, 3v3, 2v2v2v2 or 8-player free-for-all** against bots.
- **7 modes**: Rush Build, Workshop Clash, King of the Hill, Gold Rush, Combat Race, Survival and Boss Battle against a giant Titan.
- **7 arenas**: canyons, frozen islands, a lava volcano, desert mesas, a toxic swamp, a neon city and a jungle temple.
- **Parts break off one by one**: lose a wheel and you limp, lose your core and you're out on foot. Keep fighting, repair your mech, or get revenge!
- **First-person cockpit** from the Camera part you place yourself.
- **Two arenas** (Canyon Lake, Frost Isles) in Day, Sunset and Night.
- Power-ups, spike rams, dashes, daily challenges, achievements, a shop full of paints and trails.
- **Save clips with F8** and share your best moments.

Online multiplayer is in development.
