## Long description
**Build anything. Break everything.**

Cogwild is a mech-building battle game. Snap together wheels, monster-truck tires, legs, floats, propellers, wings, rotors, jets, cannons, rockets, lasers, spikes and shields, then give it a car body with a hood, cabin, fenders, spoiler and headlights. Your design decides how you fight.

🔧 **Your design is your strategy**: wheels are fast, big wheels roll over anything, legs climb cliffs, floats sail, rotors hover, wings and jets fly.
🏎 **Drives like a racing game**: steer, brake and drift while you aim with the mouse.
⭐ **Level up** from Mono to Hybrid to Omni and mix land, water and air in one mech.
▶ **One button to play**: pick an arena, press PLAY and you're matched with players there. After 30 seconds bots fill the empty seats, so you never wait long. Private rooms with invite links for friends.
🏆 **Trophies and Ranked**: win matches, unlock over 70 Trophy Road rewards, then climb Ranked from Bronze to Masters.
👥 **Friends and parties**: add friends, see who's online, team up in a party of 4 and queue together.
🕹 **8 modes**: Rush Build, Workshop Clash, King of the Hill, Gold Rush, Bounty Hunt, Combat Race, Survival and Boss Battle against a giant Titan. Plus Ranked and weekly events.
🗺 **8 arenas**: canyons, frozen islands, a lava volcano, desert mesas, a toxic swamp, a neon city, a jungle temple and a scrapyard, by day, sunset or night.
💥 **Parts break off one by one**: lose a wheel and you limp, lose your core and you fight on foot. Repair your mech in seconds or get revenge!
🎁 Daily gifts, daily challenges, achievements, a free Cog Pass and a shop full of paints, dash trails, mech finishes (chrome, gold, neon, rainbow) and wreck effects. Everything is earned by playing.
☁ **One account everywhere**: your progress follows you between the browser, Windows and Android.
🎮 Keyboard + mouse, controller and touch controls. Remap any key.

Free to play. No ads from us, no pay-to-win. Quick chat only in matches (no typing to strangers); chat and voice chat with friends, party and club, with block, report and a "Quick chat only" setting for kids.
