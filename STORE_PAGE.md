## Long description
**Build anything. Break everything.**

Cogwild is a mech-building battle game. Snap together wheels, legs, floats, propellers, wings, rotors, jets, cannons, rockets, lasers, spikes and shields. Your design decides how you fight.

🔧 **Your design is your strategy**: wheels are fast, legs climb cliffs, floats sail, rotors hover, wings and jets fly.
⭐ **Level up** from Mono to Hybrid to Omni and mix land, water and air in one mech.
▶ **One button to play**: pick an arena, press PLAY and you're matched with players there. After 30 seconds bots fill the empty seats, so you never wait long. Private rooms with invite links for friends.
🏆 **Trophies**: win matches, climb from Bronze to Legend and unlock 20 Trophy Road rewards.
👥 **Friends and parties**: add friends, see who's online, team up in a party of 4 and queue together.
🕹 **7 modes**: Rush Build, Workshop Clash, King of the Hill, Gold Rush, Combat Race, Survival and Boss Battle against a giant Titan.
🗺 **7 arenas**: canyons, frozen islands, a lava volcano, desert mesas, a toxic swamp, a neon city and a jungle temple, by day, sunset or night.
💥 **Parts break off one by one**: lose a wheel and you limp, lose your core and you fight on foot. Repair your mech or get revenge!
🎁 Daily gifts, daily challenges, 26 achievements and a shop full of paints and dash trails.
☁ **One account everywhere**: your progress follows you between the browser, Windows and Android.
🎮 Keyboard + mouse, controller and touch controls. Remap any key.

Free to play. No ads from us, no pay-to-win. Quick chat only in matches (no typing to strangers); chat and voice chat with friends, party and club, with block, report and a "Quick chat only" setting for kids.
