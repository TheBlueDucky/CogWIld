🪟 Installing on Windows

  1. Open the file you downloaded.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because Cogwild is a new game that isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Log in with your Cogwild account to bring your progress along.

🍎 Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the zip you downloaded and drag Cogwild into your Applications folder.
  2. The first time, right-click Cogwild and choose Open, then Open again (Cogwild isn't signed by Apple yet). On macOS 15 or newer: open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. If the Mac says Cogwild "is damaged", open Terminal and run xattr -cr /Applications/Cogwild.app, then open it again.

🐧 Installing on Linux

  1. Extract the .tar.gz (double-click it, or tar -xzf Cogwild-1.7.3-linux-x64.tar.gz).
  2. Open the folder and run ./cogwild. Run ./install-shortcut.sh once to add Cogwild to your app menu.

🤖 Installing on Android

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (only for this install; you can switch it off afterwards).
  3. Tap Install, open Cogwild and turn your phone sideways.
